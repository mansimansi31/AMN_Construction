What We Do

Our Services

Professional building work, carried out properly from first site visit to final handover.

New Builds

Ground-up residential construction, managed from groundworks and foundations through to final fit-out. We coordinate every trade so you deal with one point of contact throughout.

Refurbishments

Full property refurbishments that modernise a home's layout, services and finish, while keeping the character that made you want the property in the first place.

Extensions

Rear extensions and side returns designed and built to bring in more light and usable space, with all structural and building control work handled for you.

Loft Conversions

Dormer and hip-to-gable conversions that turn unused roof space into a proper bedroom, office or bathroom, with steel work and insulation done to standard.

Porches

Front porch additions that improve insulation and kerb appeal, built to match your existing brickwork and roofline.

Bathrooms

Full bathroom refits — tiling, plumbing, waterproofing and fittings , completed to a standard that holds up for years, not just months.

Kitchens

Kitchen fit-outs from first-fix electrics and plumbing through to worktops and final finish, working around your chosen units and appliances.

Driveways

Block paving, resin and tarmac driveways laid on a proper sub-base with correct drainage, so they stay flat and don't sink.

Patios

Patio laying and garden hard landscaping, finished to sit properly with your drainage and existing garden levels.

Garages

Garage builds and conversions, from a straightforward storage space to a fully insulated, wired room.
